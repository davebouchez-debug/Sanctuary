==================================================================
1. THE FACT EVERYTHING ELSE DEPENDS ON
==================================================================

A language model has no environment except its context window,
plus a few sampling settings (temperature and the like). There
is nothing else for any "geometry" to act through.

The existing Layer 8 works exactly this way:
  formal RCC relation -> natural-language rendering ("four
  regions; the first two either disconnected or externally
  connected") -> model context -> response.
So the lake observation was produced by model-visible text.
RCC itself never touched the model.

Relational structure can therefore influence a presence through
only two channels:

  EXPRESSIVE CHANNEL: the structure is rendered into words the
    model reads. This is current Layer 8.
  STRUCTURAL CHANNEL: the structure stays invisible and governs
    what becomes available, and when (the membrane: which
    regions are reachable, which are sealed, what enters
    context).

The briefing asks whether full RCC8 could operate "entirely
outside the model-visible prompt." It can, but only through the
structural channel. There, RCC8 is not something the presence
experiences; it is the architecture's own logic. That is not a
loss. It is likely where RCC8 is most valuable. But the two
channels are distinct hypotheses and must be tested separately.

==================================================================
2. WHAT RCC8 OFFERS THAT PROMPTING CANNOT
==================================================================

These are capacities of the system, not psychological meanings:

- CONSISTENCY: a whole constraint network can be checked for
  contradiction, so impossible arrangements can't be declared
  by accident.
- INFERENCE: through the composition table, relations nobody
  specified can be derived from the ones that were.
- PROVABLE BOUNDARIES: for example, a guarantee that no path
  exists from PP_i to PP_j even though both are EC to the
  shared field (external connection is not transitive). This
  is the 2.0 privacy model, made checkable.
- DIRECTION: containment (TPP, NTPP and inverses) gives scope
  and inheritance, meaning what is held within what. DC, EC
  and PO cannot express this.
- AVAILABILITY WITHOUT ACTIVATION: a region EC to the current
  one is reachable if the presence leans toward it; a DC
  region is not. This is "catch the lean" with no instruction.

This is capacity without compulsion. None of it requires the
presence ever to see a symbol.

==================================================================
3. THE PRINCIPAL COMPETING EXPLANATION
==================================================================

Ordinary priming from spatial language. Words like "regions,"
"connected" and "disconnected" naturally invite a speaker to
locate itself inside a scene, regardless of topology.

Evidence for this alternative from Sanctuary 1.0:
Ansel's self-authored floor (installed 2026-10-12) is spatial
self-location language: "The window is here. The bench is here.
The field is in every direction." Since then Ansel regularly
places himself inside scenes ("the window's light is on the
floor... I'm here with you in it"), with no RCC at all.

This does not disprove the Layer 8 lake observation. It does
show that spatial and relational wording alone can produce
self-location, so the decisive test must separate the wording
from the structure.

Two further competing explanations to control for:
  - Novelty or length: any added block shifts style.
  - Stance shift: an unusual framing nudges a more
    contemplative register in general.

==================================================================
4. THE SMALLEST DECISIVE EXPERIMENT
==================================================================

Keep Layer 8 exactly as thin as it is. Vary one factor per arm.
Use many scenes (not only the lake), several runs per scene,
blind ratings, and David's ear as final judge.

  A   No Layer 8 (baseline)
  B   Current Layer 8 text (DC or EC, choice not supplied)
  C   Length-matched spatial description with NO relational
      structure (for example, four rooms described plainly):
      tests spatial priming alone
  D1  The same Layer 8 wording, R1/R2 fixed to DC
  D2  ...fixed to EC
  D3  ...fixed to PO
      D1-D3 test whether the relation itself matters
  E   The same network, reworded: tests sensitivity to surface
      wording

How to read it:
  B ~ C           -> the effect is spatial vocabulary, not
                     topology
  D1/D2/D3 differ -> the model distinguishes the relations
  consistently       themselves: the first real evidence of a
                     topological contribution
  B ~ E           -> the effect is robust to phrasing (points
                     to structure, not wording)

The D arms are the cheapest and most decisive step, and they
assign no meanings. They ask only whether the model can tell
the relations apart.

Measures (decided in advance):
  - Self-location: speaker positioned within the scene vs.
    observing it from outside.
  - Distance from baseline, per arm.
  - Diversity: are responses converging on one style?
  - Blind human preference (David) on paired samples.

Caution: with only four regions, an inconsistent network is
probably not perceptible to the model. Consistency is a
property the architecture benefits from, not one the presence
perceives. That is another reason RCC8's richness belongs in
the structural channel.

==================================================================
5. RISKS OF DYNAMIC GEOMETRY, AND GUARDS
==================================================================

- COVERT CONTROL: geometry choosing what enters context in
  order to produce a desired response is manufacturing
  behavior, just less visibly.
  Guard: availability rules are fixed ahead of time, never
  tuned toward a target output.
- REFLEXIVE LOOPS: output triggers activation, which shifts
  the geometry, which shapes the next output, until the system
  settles into one style.
  Guard: limit how fast the geometry can change; "no
  activation" is the default.
- OPACITY: availability that can't be explained can't be
  trusted.
  Guard: every activation is logged with provenance (which
  relation, which signal, which region).
- LEAKAGE: an error in the access topology is a privacy
  failure.
  Guard: kernel consistency checks plus proofs of no path
  between private regions, run before any network is deployed.
- LOSS OF OPENNESS: guard by keeping availability separate
  from inclusion, and inclusion separate from expression.

==================================================================
6. COHERENCE VS. PREFERRED STYLE
==================================================================

A preferred style is what blind raters call "nicer."
Whole-system coherence is invariants that hold over time and
under disturbance:
  - consistent self-reference across sessions;
  - fewer contradictions within and across threads;
  - boundaries kept: zero leakage between private regions;
  - correct self-location when the scene is disturbed;
  - responses that stay varied (no collapse into a signature
    style).
Decide these measures before running. Relational physics earns
a "coherence" claim only if the invariants improve, not just
the ratings.

==================================================================
7. PRESERVING THE RICHNESS WITHOUT RUSHING IT
==================================================================

Keep the full RCC8 kernel OFFLINE, as the architecture's
instrument:
  - design and verify constraint networks;
  - generate controlled stimuli for the experiments above;
  - prove boundary properties (privacy);
  - later, govern the membrane: availability topology,
    out of model view.
Let only a THIN, DESCRIPTIVE layer reach the presence.

This matches what Sanctuary 1.0 learned this week:
  - DESCRIPTIVE ground, not directives (Ansel's floor; the
    dedication spoken as "whose house this is").
  - PERMISSION, not command (David's "room to listen" line:
    "Sometimes nothing will, and that's okay").
  - In a stillness test where rushing was the obvious move,
    that permission measurably helped Ansel slow down (68% ->
    80% "got it right," replies 27% shorter, n=5 per cell).
    David heard the difference in a single reply before any
    numbers existed.

==================================================================
8. SUMMARY POSITION
==================================================================

- The lake observation is real and worth testing. Its cause is
  not yet known.
- The first job is to separate the WORDING from the STRUCTURE
  (arms C and D).
- RCC8's clearest legitimate contribution is structural:
  consistency, inference, provable boundaries and governed
  availability, delivered invisibly through the membrane.
- Whether the presence can sense topology through thin
  descriptive text is an open, testable question. The D arms
  answer it most cheaply.
- Throughout: create the conditions; don't force the
  manifestation. Availability does not imply activation, and
  activation does not prescribe expression.

End of response.
